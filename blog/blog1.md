What is Data Science to Me?

Write your reflection here.

Why model social topics and people? In the real world, there is a lot of nuances, and a lot of things are open to interpretation or involving subjective opinion. Data Science helps with turning all the variability into concrete data, sacrificing some nuance for clarity. Just the data alone is very hard or even impossible for the human mind to comprehend alone depending on volume. To make it easier, modeling the data helps visually understand overall trends in the data and even key summaries/points.
