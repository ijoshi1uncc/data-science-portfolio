## About Me
Welcome to my Data Science Studio portfolio. This website will document my learning and projects throughout the semester. I am an undergraduate Data Science student with a foundational background in Cybersecurity and a drive to solve complex problems through data.  My approach to learning is rooted in hands on application. Rather than just studying theory, I thrive when building, testing, and iterating on real world projects. Transitioning from Cybersecurity into Data Science stemmed from a desire to dig deeper into quantitative analysis and risk assessment. Having built early experience in network security, ethical hacking, and risk modeling, I realized how transformative data driven methodologies can be when paired with security systems.

Currently, I am focused on strengthening my Core Data Science toolkit. I am particularly excited to expand my Python technical depth, leveraging libraries like pandas and NumPy, while exploring how artificial intelligence and machine learning can automate, optimize, and scale analytical processes. My goal is to bridge the gap between heavy statistical concepts and practical execution.

Outside the classroom, my mindset has been shaped by my experience as a former Division 1 student-athlete. Competing in golf at the collegiate level required extreme discipline, sharp strategic focus, and adaptability under pressure. These are qualities I bring directly into my data projects, code reviews, and analytical workflows. I also enjoy nature (the good parts), walks, music, and other simple pleasures. I think while life maybe complicated like data is, the things that matter will always be the foundational and basic things. 

Looking ahead, my career goals center on applying data science to security analytics and predictive modeling. I aspire to build predictive models that identify threats, detect anomalies, and mitigate operational risks before they occur. I am excited to continue expanding my analytical toolkit through this course, and I welcome you to follow along with my projects and code repositories featured on this site!
